# Underscore

Rivals ship weekly. Threats land overnight. Underscore keeps your apps evolving toward your goal.

## How it works

- **Watch.** Underscore follows real visits to your live site and notes where people get stuck.
- **Draft.** It writes a new version of the page aimed at what it found.
- **Try.** The version runs beside the live page and is judged against the goal you set.
- **Hold the line.** Red lines you choose stop any change that would cross them.
