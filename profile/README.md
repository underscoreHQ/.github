# Underscore

**Energising Industry in Booming Cities**

We build energy coordination software for industrial manufacturers in emerging markets. Our intelligent devices plug into factory equipment to optimise power sources and maintain production during grid failures.

## What We Do

Industrial manufacturers in emerging markets lose around 20% of production due to power instability. We solve this with:

- **Local Intelligence** — Compact hardware running on-site with no cloud dependency
- **Demand Forecasting** — 85%+ accuracy predicting energy needs hours or days ahead
- **Smart Coordination** — Optimised allocation across grid, diesel, solar, and battery sources
- **Network Capability** — Connect multiple facilities to collectively negotiate better power rates

## Results

- 25%+ diesel cost reduction
- 85%+ demand prediction accuracy
- 4-hour adaptation to grid changes
- Prevention of production loss from energy failures
